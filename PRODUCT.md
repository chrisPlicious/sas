# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

SvelteKit (Svelte 5, TypeScript). User-chosen (switched from Next.js). Static-friendly marketing site, prerendered; no CMS. Inquiry form is front-end only for now (validation + UI states); delivery to be wired later.

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally:

1. **Builders:** Cebu homeowners and small business owners planning a new house, multi-storey residence, townhouse, light commercial space, renovation, or needing plans signed/sealed and permits processed. They arrive with a lot, an idea, or existing plans and want to know SAS can take it from plan to finish at a price that fits their budget.
2. **Renters:** contractors, subcontractors, and private homebuilders across Cebu who need construction machinery and tools for a job. They want to see what is available and reach SAS fast.

## Product Purpose

The first standalone website for SAS Construction Services and Equipment Rentals – Cebu (until now the business has lived only on Facebook). It must let either audience understand what SAS does within seconds and start an inquiry by phone/SMS or the inquiry form. Success = more qualified inquiries (ocular inspection requests, quotation requests, rental requests).

## Positioning

A local, family-run Cebu contractor (SAS = the Sasing family name) that covers the whole job under one roof: design and plans, sign & seal, permit processing, structural build, finishing trades, and the equipment itself. Rental and building live in the same business, so the builder who pours your foundation also rents you the mixer.

## Operating Context

- Base: Enriquez Compound, Atillo Street, Punta Princesa, Cebu City. Service area: Cebu City, Mandaue, Talisay, and surrounding Cebu Province municipalities.
- Inquiries accepted 24 hours (messaging, calls, appointment requests).
- Real workflow: Inquiry (lot specs, idea, or plans) → Ocular inspection on site (ground conditions, road access, orientation) → Bill of Materials & quotation built to the client's budget → Build, with progress videos/walkthroughs posted for the client.
- Pricing (construction and rental) is quoted per project; there is no public rate sheet.

## Product Principles

1. **Two doors, one yard.** Building and renting are equal front doors; neither is buried.
2. **Show the process, not boasts.** Trust comes from the real workflow (ocular → BOM → build → progress updates), not inflated numbers.
3. **Every page ends at a phone number.** Inquiry is always one tap away.
4. **Honest by construction.** Only verifiable facts; placeholders are visibly marked until replaced.
5. **Local and specific.** Cebu places, Cebu sites, the Sasing family's own business.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
